03

How to apply

Do not send only “I want to join.”

That does not give us enough to review.

  1. 01

    Prove your skill.

    Send a sanitized, redacted example of your work. No targets, credentials, or live data — only proof of your process and ability.

  2. 02

    Encrypt your message.

    Use our PGP key. Unencrypted applications will not be reviewed.

  3. 03

    Be concise.

    Tell us:

    • What you can do
    • What tools you use
    • What you want from us
  4. 04

    Wait.

    If we are interested, you will receive a response within seven days through a secure channel. If you hear nothing, there is no current fit.
