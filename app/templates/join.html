01

Sektor-7 is not an open community or a general chat server.

We are a focused collective of security researchers, developers, and infrastructure specialists who value discretion, authorization, and demonstrated skill.

If you are reading this page, you may be curious — or you may have the experience we are looking for.

03

How to apply

Do not send only “I want to join.”

That does not give us enough to review.

  1. 01

    Prove your skill.

    Give a sanitized, redacted summary of your work. No targets, credentials, live data, private keys, or client material.

  2. 02

    Keep the first message sanitized.

    Use the account-linked form below for the initial application. Use our PGP key only when sensitive follow-up is necessary.

  3. 03

    Be concise.

    Tell us what you can do, what tools you use, and what you want from us.

  4. 04

    Wait.

    If we are interested, you will receive a response through the reply channel you provide.
