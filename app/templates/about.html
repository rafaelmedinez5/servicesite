About us

Security work should begin with clarity.

Servicesite connects focused security engagements with a private purchasing flow. Every service is presented with a clear description, listed price, and an authorization boundary that remains separate from payment.

01

Authorized by design

Payment never grants permission to test a system. Written scope and authorization are reviewed before work begins.

02

Evidence over noise

Engagements are centered on useful findings, understandable impact, and practical recommendations.

03

Privacy with restraint

The site avoids unnecessary trackers and keeps payment, account, and operational boundaries deliberately separated.

Our boundary

A marketplace is not an authorization document.

Servicesite makes it easier to discover and purchase a defined security service. It does not replace scoping, ownership verification, rules of engagement, or written permission.

That separation protects clients, practitioners, and the integrity of the work.
